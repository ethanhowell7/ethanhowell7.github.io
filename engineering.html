Projects

Guitar pedal builds

Role: Designer & builder Tools: Perfboard, soldering, circuit design Status: Ongoing

Pedals are where I first got comfortable with analog circuits outside a textbook. I build from schematics, populate perfboard by hand, and spend most of my time after soldering working out why a circuit isn't behaving the way the diagram says it should. It's taught me more about how transistors and op-amps actually behave under real conditions than any lecture has.

photo

Competition robot design — ARC Lightning

Team: ARC Lightning, FTC #4410 Role: Mechanical lead Peak: FTC World Championship

I spent three seasons on ARC Lightning as mechanical lead, responsible for drivetrain and scoring mechanism design within FTC's limits on size, weight, and build time. Our junior-year season took us to the FTC World Championship. Most of the real engineering happened in iteration: build a version, test it against the actual game rules, then rework whatever fails first.

Semiconductor device research

Interest: SiC / GaN & silicon devices Department: NC State ECE

I'm working to get involved in wide-bandgap and silicon device research within NC State's Electrical and Computer Engineering department, which plays a central role in the CHIPS-funded CLAWS Microelectronics Commons Hub and in PowerAmerica. Power devices are the part of EE I find most interesting right now — small design decisions with direct, measurable physical consequences.
