Projects

Photo

Guitar pedal builds

Role: Designer & builder Tools: Soldering, perfboard, circuit design Status: Ongoing

Pedals are where I first got comfortable with analog circuits outside a textbook. I build from schematics, populate perfboard by hand, and spend most of my time after soldering figuring out why a circuit isn't behaving the way the diagram says it should. It's taught me more about how transistors and op-amps actually behave under real conditions than any lecture has.

Photo

ARC Sandstorm — robot build

Team: ARC Sandstorm #24256 Role: Founder & mechanical lead Status: Active

As mechanical lead I made most of the calls on the robot's drivetrain and scoring mechanism for our first competitive season, working within FTC's usual limits on weight, size, and build time. Most of the real engineering happened in iteration — build a version, test it against the actual game rules, rework whatever fails first.
